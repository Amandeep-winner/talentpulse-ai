# TalentPulse Comprehensive Recruitment Policy

## 1. Policy Overview and Purpose
This document defines the standard recruitment policy for Demo Talent Co and all subsidiary operating entities.
The purpose of this policy is to establish transparent, equitable, and efficient hiring practices across all talent acquisition pipelines.
Every recruitment cycle must align with organizational standards regarding integrity, candidate dignity, and data privacy.
Hiring teams must adhere strictly to the protocols defined in this document without exception.

## 2. Requisition Approval and Headcount Planning
All new open positions require explicit headcount authorization before any external job post can be published.
Department heads must submit a formal requisition detailing business rationale, salary band, grade level, and required skill taxonomy.
The Head of People and the Chief Financial Officer must approve the requisition within five business days.
Unapproved requisitions shall not be broadcast to programmatic publishers or listed on the public career site.
Emergency backfill requisitions follow an expedited forty-eight hour executive approval pipeline.

## 3. Hiring Stages and Standard Workflow
The recruitment lifecycle follows a defined five-stage progression to ensure objective evaluation.
The initial stage begins with automated and recruiter-assisted application screening.
Candidates who meet core criteria advance to the structured screening interview conducted by the talent acquisition team.
The subsequent technical or functional assessment validates domain expertise and job-specific problem-solving capabilities.
Finalist candidates participate in a panel debrief interview focusing on team collaboration and leadership principles.
Upon unanimous consensus, the talent partner issues a standardized offer package endorsed by the department head.

## 4. Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
Talent acquisition teams and hiring managers are bound by organizational response time standards.
All incoming applications must receive initial review within three business days of submission.
Candidates completing an interview round must receive constructive feedback or stage progression updates within forty-eight hours.
Hiring managers must submit written evaluation scorecards within twenty-four hours of conducting an interview session.
Requisition time-to-fill targets are calibrated at thirty business days for individual contributors and forty-five days for leadership positions.

## 5. Candidate Privacy and Data Confidentiality
Candidate personal data constitutes strictly confidential information protected under global data protection regulations.
Resumes, contact details, and evaluation notes must reside exclusively within the TalentPulse system.
Under no circumstances should candidate materials be shared via unencrypted personal email or external file transfers.
Candidate information is accessible solely to authorized recruiters, hiring managers, and interview panellists assigned to the requisition.
All candidate records are retained in compliance with statutory retention schedules and encrypted at rest.

## 6. Background Verification and Pre-Employment Checks
All formal offers of employment are contingent upon the satisfactory completion of background verification.
Verification parameters include historical employment validation, highest degree educational credentials, and criminal record screening.
Any material misrepresentation or intentional omission discovered during verification constitutes grounds for immediate rescission of an offer.
Background reports are audited biannually by legal counsel to ensure consistent compliance with labor regulations.

## 7. Compensation and Offer Governance
Compensation proposals must fall within the predetermined salary band established during requisition creation.
Any out-of-band offer exception requires documented justification and written authorization from the Chief Executive Officer.
Sign-on bonuses, relocation allowances, and equity grants adhere to standard tier policies based on job grade.
Internal equity audits are performed quarterly to verify that offer trends maintain parity across comparable roles.
Offers remain open for acceptance for a maximum period of seven business days unless an extension is granted in writing.
