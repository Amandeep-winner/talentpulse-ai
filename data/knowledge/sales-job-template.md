# TalentPulse Commercial and Enterprise Sales Hiring Framework

## 1. Sales Organization Charter and Mission
The commercial sales organization at Demo Talent Co drives enterprise customer acquisition, contract expansion, and long-term revenue partnerships.
Our sales philosophy prioritizes consultative solution selling, deep industry empathy, and quantifiable client return on investment.
Hiring the right sales professionals requires assessing both aggressive hunting instincts and sophisticated executive stakeholder management.
Standardized templates and scoring rubrics ensure consistent evaluation of sales acumen across regional teams and enterprise tiers.

## 2. Sales Role Archetypes and Experience Profiles
We distinguish between four distinct sales functions across the customer acquisition and retention lifecycle.
Business Development Representatives (BDRs) specialize in outbound prospecting, multi-channel outreach, and qualification of enterprise leads.
Account Executives (AEs) manage full sales cycles from initial discovery to commercial proposal presentation and contract closing.
Enterprise Strategic Directors handle complex, multi-stakeholder deals exceeding substantial annual contract values and multi-year commitments.
Customer Success and Account Managers focus on product adoption, expansion pipelines, gross retention, and annual renewal negotiations.

## 3. Core Competency Dimensions for Sales Talent
Evaluation of sales candidates examines five essential competencies foundational to sustainable high performance.
The first competency evaluates discovery mastery, measuring how effectively a candidate uncovers client pain points and budget constraints.
The second competency assesses consultative presentation skills, evaluating the ability to tie product capabilities directly to business value.
The third competency evaluates objection handling and commercial negotiation, specifically preserving margin without compromising client goodwill.
The fourth competency measures pipeline hygiene, CRM discipline, and rigor in sales forecasting methodologies.
The fifth competency assesses grit, resilience, and personal accountability in recovering from lost opportunities or market headwinds.

## 4. Practical Sales Simulation and Pitch Evaluation
Every sales interview process incorporates a practical commercial simulation tailored to our target market.
Candidates receive a realistic company profile, product overview, and fictional client scenario forty-eight hours prior to the session.
During the simulation, interview panellists roleplay senior executive buyers representing divergent technical, financial, and operational concerns.
The candidate must conduct a twenty-minute discovery session followed by a concise ten-minute executive value pitch.
Panellists score the candidate on active listening, question sequencing, executive presence, and effective transition to agreed next steps.
Unprepared monologues, defensive reactions to pricing pushback, or aggressive pressuring tactics result in automatic disqualification.

## 5. Quota Verification and Historical Performance Review
Sales resumes frequently contain unverified performance claims that require diligent empirical substantiation.
Candidates must provide documented context regarding their historical quotas, average deal sizes, and average sales cycle lengths.
Interviewers must explore the proportion of quota attained through inbound marketing leads versus self-generated outbound prospecting.
Reference checks with former sales leaders verify individual contribution, team collaboration, and adherence to company ethics.
Compensation plans are structured with competitive base salaries paired with uncapped commissions tied to verified revenue realization.
