# TalentPulse Engineering Hiring Framework and Job Requirements Template

## 1. Engineering Philosophy and Role Archetypes
Demo Talent Co builds high-throughput, mission-critical distributed systems requiring rigorous engineering discipline.
Our engineering hiring strategy focuses on foundational problem solving, architectural maturity, and verifiable execution ability.
We categorize engineering positions across four specialized tracks: Backend Systems, Frontend Infrastructure, Fullstack Applications, and Site Reliability Engineering.
Job descriptions must articulate core challenges, technology stacks, operational expectations, and measurable success criteria.
Hiring teams must maintain standardized requirement templates to ensure consistency across hiring managers and geographies.

## 2. Engineering Competency Matrix and Level Definitions
Engineering levels reflect progressive scopes of technical complexity, autonomy, architectural influence, and team mentorship.
Junior Engineers (Level E1) demonstrate solid proficiency in language syntax, clean unit testing, and prompt bug triage under senior guidance.
Mid-level Engineers (Level E2) execute non-trivial feature milestones independently, draft concise technical proposals, and debug production anomalies.
Senior Engineers (Level E3) architect resilient microservices, establish team engineering best practices, and mentor junior colleagues effectively.
Staff Engineers (Level E4) formulate multi-quarter architectural roadmaps, eliminate cross-team bottlenecks, and establish cross-functional consensus.
Principal Engineers (Level E5) influence enterprise technology strategy, govern foundational platforms, and resolve high-risk systemic failures.

## 3. Standard Technical Evaluation Stages
The technical evaluation consists of calibrated assessments designed to simulate realistic day-to-day engineering workflows.
Stage one evaluates practical coding proficiency through a live pair-programming session focused on data structures and modularity.
Candidates may choose modern languages such as TypeScript, Go, Java, or Python according to role specifications.
Algorithmic trivia and puzzle-based riddles are strictly prohibited in favor of realistic business domain problems.
Stage two evaluates distributed systems design, data modeling, concurrency management, and failure recovery strategies.
Senior candidates are expected to discuss database indexing, event streams, caching tradeoffs, and horizontal scaling mechanics.
Stage three consists of a deep-dive code review and architecture debrief reviewing past complex systems delivered by the candidate.

## 4. Required and Preferred Skill Taxonomy
Job descriptions must clearly distinguish between non-negotiable prerequisites and preferred supplemental capabilities.
Prerequisites represent mandatory day-one competencies without which a candidate cannot perform essential role obligations.
Preferred capabilities represent accelerators that enhance velocity but can be acquired through on-the-job training.
Backend roles require mastery of relational databases, REST and gRPC API design, microservices architecture, and message brokers.
Frontend roles require deep knowledge of modern frameworks like React and Next.js, web accessibility, bundle optimization, and state management.
Site Reliability Engineering roles require expertise in Linux internals, Kubernetes orchestration, Terraform infrastructure as code, and observability platforms.

## 5. Candidate Experience and Work Sample Guidelines
Assessments must respect candidate time investments while providing genuine insight into practical engineering abilities.
Take-home assignments, when utilized, must be strictly scoped to require no more than three hours of total effort.
Candidates completing take-home projects receive detailed technical feedback regardless of advancement decisions.
Panellists must ensure collaborative, supportive interview environments where candidates can think aloud and validate assumptions.
Accommodations including assistive technology, flexible scheduling, and extended evaluation time must be granted promptly upon request.
